# ✈️ Skytravel

Skytravel is a travel and tourism website designed to help users explore different destinations, discover travel packages, and plan their trips in one place.

The website provides destination information, booking options, travel services, a photo gallery, customer reviews, and a contact section.

## 🌍 Features

- 🏠 **Home Page** – Welcome section with a travel video slider.
- 📝 **Travel Booking** – Users can enter their destination, number of guests, arrival date, and leaving date.
- 📦 **Travel Packages** – Explore packages for destinations such as:
  - Kedarnath
  - Agra
  - Mumbai
  - Goa
  - Lucknow
  - Dwarka
- 🏨 **Travel Services** – Includes information about:
  - Affordable hotels
  - Food and drinks
  - Safety guides
  - Worldwide travel
  - Fast travel
  - Adventure activities
- 🖼️ **Gallery** – Displays images of different travel destinations.
- ⭐ **Reviews** – Users can submit reviews along with their location and rating.
- 📞 **Contact Form** – Users can send their name, email, mobile number, subject, and message.
- 🔐 **Login Form** – Provides a login interface for users.
- 📱 **Responsive Navigation** – Includes a menu bar and navigation links for different sections.
- 🎥 **Video Slider** – Uses multiple travel videos on the home section.
- 🔄 **Swiper Slider** – Used for reviews and brand sections.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Structure of the website
- **CSS3** – Styling and layout
- **JavaScript** – Website interactions
- **PHP** – Booking, reviews, and contact form processing
- **Swiper.js** – Sliders and carousels
- **Font Awesome** – Icons
- **MySQL/PHP Backend** – Used by the connected PHP files for form-related functionality
